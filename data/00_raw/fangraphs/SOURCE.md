**Vendor/URL:** FanGraphs (fangraphs.com) Major League leaderboards, exported by hand as CSV.

**Export date:** 11-17-2026

**Export settings:**
- Seasons 2000–2026, one row per player per season.
- All players: no qualified or minimum PA/IP filter. Batting PA starts at 0 and pitching IP at 0.0.
- Seasons are split by team. A player traded mid-season has one row per club, and there is no combined row.
